## Grounding principle

Every continuous structure in the repository is either tied to a hypergraph by a theorem with content or is the target of an explicitly OPEN convergence statement ([`BRIDGE_MACHINERY.md`](BRIDGE_MACHINERY.md)). Several statements in the library do not meet this: the origin obligations, the rate obligations and the `Milestone` Props hold for every target and are VACUOUS (see above). `ContinuumLimitStatement.lean` contains statements guarded by `∧ False`, which have no proof.
